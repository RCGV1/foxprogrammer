FoxProgrammer Support

FoxProgrammer is a community project for PicoFox settings and audio on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Getting started
Use Settings to choose the PicoFox folder through the system picker. Confirm the drive appears in Files or Finder first. Use Audio to record, import, create a tone or generate speech, then play and trim it. Settings and audio have separate Save and Export actions.

External drives
A data-capable USB cable and a compatible mounted volume are required. If the drive disconnects, local edits are kept and direct saves are disabled. Reconnect the drive or export locally. Use the device manufacturer’s instructions to apply saved settings. FoxProgrammer does not flash firmware or repair filesystems.

Audio
Full quality previews the edited source; PicoFox mode previews device bandwidth. Device exports use 5 kHz mono PCM16 WAV. Speech starts with normalization and reduced gain. Restore default audio replaces editor audio and may save to an available selected folder after confirmation; retain backups before replacing files.

Report a problem
https://github.com/RCGV1/foxprogrammer/issues
Include your app version, operating-system version and steps to reproduce. Describe the cable/adapter and hardware revision when relevant. Do not post private recordings, full device backups or identifying information in public issues.
