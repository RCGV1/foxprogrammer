FoxProgrammer Privacy Policy
Effective October 29, 2026

FoxProgrammer is a community project for preparing PicoFox settings and audio.

Data collection
FoxProgrammer does not send recordings, speech text, callsigns, settings, or usage data to a developer-operated service. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, user account, or cloud backend.

Local processing and storage
Audio editing, tone creation and file conversion run on your device. Speech uses Apple’s system speech services and available voices. The app keeps working audio and edits in memory. Settings drafts, remembered folder access and safety backups may be stored locally until you remove them. Backups can include original files, source audio and edit settings.

Permissions
Microphone access is requested when you choose Record. Folder access is granted through the system picker and is used to read or save the selected PicoFox files. You can revoke microphone or folder access in system privacy settings. Revoking access does not delete existing local backups or exported files.

Files and exports
You decide which files to import, where to export, and when to save to PicoFox. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles those files under its own terms and privacy policy. Opening public support or license links uses your browser; GitHub and other website operators may process connection information under their policies.

Retention and deletion
Unsaved editor audio is discarded when the app exits. Local drafts and backups remain until deleted. On iPhone/iPad, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading does not. On Mac, quit the app and remove its sandbox container at ~/Library/Containers/app.picofox.studio.mac to remove its local data. Save any backups you wish to keep first. Files exported elsewhere or saved on the radio remain under your control and are not removed by deleting app data.

Contact and changes
Questions and deletion guidance: https://github.com/RCGV1/foxprogrammer/issues
Do not post recordings, private callsigns, device backups or other sensitive material in public issues. Policy changes will be published on this page.
